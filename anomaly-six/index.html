INSTRUCTIONS FOR AN IMPOSSIBLE OBJECT

Nothing to see. Six things to find.

This is a miniature cabinet of six moving, procedurally generated curiosities. In Preview, drag the object to orbit it, tap to provoke a reaction, and choose the six numbered exhibits.

In Cube Camera, the specimens appear anchored to the faces of your MERGE Cube. The first run can build tracking data from MERGE’s official PDF automatically. No photography is necessary for a standard cube. Alternatively, learn an unusual cube using your own photographs.

If using personal photos, fill the guide with each face and use diffuse light. This is experimental image tracking, not MERGE's proprietary cube-tracking engine. It may lose tracking when faces rotate sharply, and it cannot infer the whole cube's rigid orientation from one face.

Tap Disturb or the center of the view to unsettle an exhibit. The Improbability control changes its behavior. Sound is optional. The app requires HTTPS for the camera and an internet connection to load its open-source AR libraries.
