LightHouse

An illuminated digital play surface that works directly with physical Looney Pyramids. The screen becomes part board, part light source, part interface, with physical calibration, stacking and nesting, collision behavior, saved boards, and browser and native-device versions.

Anaglyph & Friends

Turns one ordinary photograph into stereoscopic and autostereoscopic formats using a monocular depth estimate: anaglyphs, stereograms, wiggle-grams, lenticular output, phantograms, viewer formats, print tools, and more.

Wisdom Watch

A calendar made to put an idea from a wise thinker in front of you late at night, instead of letting tomorrow's first logistical obligation own that space. Subscribe to it or import the calendar file and the entries appear alongside your ordinary events.
