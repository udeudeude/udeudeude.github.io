# Relics and Curiosities

Eighty objects with lives of their own: a key with no teeth, a jar of Thursday, a coin that refuses certain purchases, and a page whose ending changes at twilight. Each may be a treasure, a warning, or simply a question someone has carried too long.

Seek to any point during the encounters. If a scene has already begun, use the next one you hear from its beginning. Short pauses separate each encounter; the interpretation belongs to your table.
