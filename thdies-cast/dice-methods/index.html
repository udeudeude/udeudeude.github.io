DICE EDITION

D20 · Shuffled Sets

360 balanced outcomes · 30 minutes of results

Listen or download · Full written show notes

How this edition works

METHOD: EIGHTEEN SHUFFLED SETS The numbers 1–20 are shuffled separately 18 times. Each 20-result block therefore contains every face once, and each face appears exactly 18 times in the full set of 360 five-second time slots (30:00 of results). For uniformly selected time slots, each face has exactly 18/360 = 1/20 = 5% probability.

Sequential caveat: no result repeats inside its 20-result block; only a block boundary can have adjacent equal faces. This is NOT a succession of independent D20 rolls. Comparing against the giant shuffle and pair-balanced editions shows what that constraint does.

DICE EDITION

D20 · One Giant Shuffle

360 balanced outcomes · 30 minutes of results

Listen or download · Full written show notes

How this edition works

METHOD: ONE GIANT BALANCED SHUFFLE Start with 18 copies of every face 1–20, yielding 360 positions; shuffle the entire 360-entry pool just once. Each face still appears exactly 18 times, and a uniformly chosen time slot has a 5% chance of producing any given face. Unlike the 20-result blocks, identical values can occur consecutively anywhere, so it feels more like ordinary rolls.

Sequential caveat: once outcomes are selected from a finite pool, remaining frequencies change. Adjacent results are not mathematically independent; pairs are not deliberately balanced either. Global equality, not local independence, is what is guaranteed.

DICE EDITION

D20 · Every Possible Pair

400 balanced outcomes · 33 minutes of results

Listen or download · Full written show notes

How this edition works

METHOD: ORDER-TWO DE BRUIJN / RANDOMIZED EULERIAN CIRCUIT Construct a circular sequence of 400 results from an Eulerian traversal of a directed graph with one edge for each ordered pair of D20 faces (20 × 20 = 400). Every possible ordered consecutive pair, including doubles such as 7→7, appears exactly once as the sequence wraps from its last result back to its first. Each individual face therefore occurs exactly 20 times; its probability on a uniformly sampled time slot is 20/400 = 5%. Four hundred slots at five seconds total 33:20 of dice results.

The pair guarantee is *cyclic*: the final→first pair counts mathematically, but ordinary file playback passes through the closing coda and introduction instead of jumping directly from the last number to the first. To inspect that pair, skip the spoken material on repeat. The construction balances all two-roll patterns, not three-roll patterns, and still isn't a source of fresh independent randomness.

DICE EDITION

2D6 · Thirty-Six Possibilities

360 balanced outcomes · 30 minutes of results

Listen or download · Full written show notes

How this edition works

METHOD: TEN SHUFFLES OF ALL THIRTY-SIX ORDERED 2D6 PAIRS Two distinguishable six-sided dice have 6 × 6 = 36 equally likely ordered pairs: (1,2) and (2,1) are different pairs although they have the same sum. Shuffle all 36 ordered pairs independently for each of ten blocks. That gives 360 results occupying five seconds apiece, or 30:00 of functional audio. The narrator says the first die, the second die, and then the sum.

Each ordered pair appears exactly ten times. The sums 2–12 therefore occur 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10 times respectively. Their probabilities are 1,2,3,4,5,6,5,4,3,2,1 divided by 36. Seven is six times as likely as two or twelve, as it should be. Within a 36-pair block, the same ordered pair cannot repeat, but the same sum can. Consequently, the result sequence is balanced, not sequentially independent.

DICE EDITION

D100 · Three Complete Shuffles

300 balanced outcomes · 30 minutes of results

Listen or download · Full written show notes

How this edition works

METHOD: THREE SHUFFLED COMPLETE SETS OF ONE HUNDRED Shuffle 1–100 independently three times and concatenate those permutations. This gives 300 six-second result slots, exactly 30:00 of outcomes. Every integer 1–100 occurs exactly three times in the full recording, so a uniformly selected time slot produces any particular value with probability 3/300 = 1/100 = 1%. One hundred is a valid result; zero is not.

Within each 100-value set no number repeats. A repeated value may occur at a boundary between sets. That restriction differs from independent live D100 throws, but unlike the previous 300-independent-draw D100 pilot, this balanced edition cannot omit any of its 100 faces.

DICE EDITION

D4 · Four Paths

360 balanced outcomes · 30 minutes of results

Listen or download · Full written show notes

How this edition works

D4: NINETY COMPLETE SHUFFLES OF FOUR Each of ninety blocks includes 1, 2, 3 and 4 once in randomized order. There are 360 five-second slots, exactly thirty minutes of results, and each face occurs ninety times: 90/360 = 25%. Equal slot durations give exact global outcome balance for uniform time sampling within the results section. Sequential results are not independent: no face repeats inside a block, although it can repeat across a block boundary.

How this edition works

D8: FORTY-FIVE COMPLETE SHUFFLES OF EIGHT Each block contains every face 1 through 8 exactly once in randomized order. Forty-five blocks give 360 five-second result slots, thirty minutes total. Each face occurs exactly 45 times: 45/360 = 12.5%. This enforces outcome balance rather than independence of adjacent results. How this edition works

D12: THIRTY COMPLETE SHUFFLES OF TWELVE Each block contains the faces 1 through 12 exactly once in randomized order. Thirty blocks yield 360 five-second slots, thirty minutes of results. Each face appears 30 times, for an ideal probability of 30/360 = 1/12 (about 8.33%) when choosing a slot uniformly. No result repeats within a twelve-slot block, unlike independent rolling.
