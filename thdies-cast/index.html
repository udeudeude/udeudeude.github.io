01 D20 · Original shuffled-block experiment

Thirty minutes of twenty-sided die results. Seek to a random point and use the next complete number. Each set of twenty faces is shuffled before the next. This produces exact overall frequency balance but prevents repeats within each set, unlike independent rolls.

Runtime: 30:37 · Direct MP3

02 2D6 · Original independent-draw experiment

Two independent six-sided dice, followed by their sum. Seek to a random point and use the next complete result. The two dice were generated independently, but the fixed 300-roll recording is not perfectly balanced. For seeking, prefer the new complete-pair edition.

Runtime: 30:36 · Direct MP3

03 D100 · Original independent-draw experiment

One uniformly generated number from 1 through 100 at a time. Seek to a random point and take the next complete value. This early fixed recording omits six possible values (39, 43, 46, 49, 82, and 85). For seeking, prefer the new complete-shuffle edition.

Runtime: 30:36 · Direct MP3

04 The Wilderness Oracle

Eighty original, independent wilderness encounters, each with a subtle boundary cue. Seek and wait for the next complete encounter. Printed numbers and titles are not spoken.

Runtime: 31:13 · Direct MP3

05 Ten Minutes to Disaster

An original ten-minute suspense soundtrack and functional countdown. It begins with playback, provides a few time warnings, and ends with a decisive bell at exactly ten minutes.

Runtime: 10:16 · Direct MP3

All recordings are working pilots, not locked game rules. Choose the next complete result when seeking. No narration of encounter numbers or titles. Audio is original or generated from the project's own prose.
