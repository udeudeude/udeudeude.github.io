New: eight balanced dice editions

Three different D20 methods, exact-balanced 2D6 and D100, and new D4, D8, and D12 recordings. Every edition has a distinct Brother Rubicon observation followed by “I am Brother Rubicon. The die’s cast.”

Listen to the eight balanced dice editions

The original D20 is a valid balanced shuffled-set edition. The early 2D6 and D100 recordings remain as clearly labeled archival experiments, with their original audio preserved. The Wilderness Oracle and Ten Minutes to Disaster are unchanged.

D20 · Shuffled Sets

Thirty minutes of twenty-sided die results. Seek to a random point while the rolls are being spoken. Ignore any number already in progress. Your roll is the next number heard from its beginning. Each set of twenty faces is shuffled before the next. This produces exact overall frequency balance but prevents repeats within each set, unlike independent rolls.

Runtime: 30:37 · Direct MP3

2D6 · Archival Independent Draws

Two independent six-sided dice, followed by their sum. Seek to a random point during the rolls. Ignore a roll already in progress, then take the next pair of dice and its spoken sum. The two dice were generated independently, but the fixed 300-roll recording is not perfectly balanced. For seeking, prefer the new complete-pair edition.

Runtime: 30:36 · Direct MP3

D100 · Archival Independent Draws

One uniformly generated number from 1 through 100 at a time. Seek to a random point while the numbers are being spoken. Ignore any number already in progress. Your roll is the next number heard from its beginning. This early fixed recording omits six possible values (39, 43, 46, 49, 82, and 85). For seeking, prefer the new complete-shuffle edition.

Runtime: 30:36 · Direct MP3

The Wilderness Oracle

Eighty original, independent wilderness encounters, each with a subtle boundary cue. Seek during the encounters. If one has already begun, disregard it. Use the next encounter you hear from its beginning. Printed numbers and titles are not spoken.

Runtime: 31:13 · Direct MP3

Ten Minutes to Disaster

An original ten-minute suspense soundtrack and functional countdown. It begins with playback, provides a few time warnings, and ends with a decisive bell at exactly ten minutes.

Runtime: 10:16 · Direct MP3

Creatures Behaving Strangely

Twenty original encounters featuring animals acting in surprising, sometimes unsettling ways. Seek to a random point, ignore any encounter already underway, and listen for the next complete encounter after its chime.

Runtime: 12:15 · Direct MP3 · Show notes

All recordings are working pilots, not locked game rules. When seeking to a dice result, disregard anything already underway and use the next result heard from its beginning. No narration of encounter numbers or titles. Audio is original or generated from the project's own prose.
