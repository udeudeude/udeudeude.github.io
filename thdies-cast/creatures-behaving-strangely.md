# Creatures Behaving Strangely

Eighty strange encounters with animals, from the merely curious to the quietly impossible. A wolf delivers firewood. Bees arrange seals inside a clock. A deer counts travelers at a bridge. What these things mean belongs to the people who find them.

To use: Seek to a random point during the encounters. If an encounter is already underway, use the next complete one. Brief pauses separate the scenes. If the recording ends, return to the beginning of the encounters.
