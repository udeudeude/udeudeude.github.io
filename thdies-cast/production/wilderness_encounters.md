# THE WILDERNESS ORACLE
## Eighty independent encounters — first manuscript

Each entry is a self-contained spoken scene. In the podcast, do **not** read the numbers or titles. A listener seeking randomly may land halfway through any entry; the rule is to listen for the **next complete encounter** after playback resumes. Keep entries discrete, separated by an audible but unobtrusive neutral cue and silence, and never have one entry refer back to another. These are encounters rather than predetermined combats: their significance belongs to the group.

### 01 | The Borrowed Shadows
On a bare stretch of road, the party's shadows suddenly point toward the sun. A traveling mason kneels beside his own shadow, measuring it with string. He claims this happens before earthquakes, but he has been waiting here for three days without feeling a tremor.

### 02 | The Orchard of Bells
Beyond a broken gate grows an apple orchard where every fruit contains a small bronze bell. They ring when the wind rises. An elderly gardener offers fruit in exchange for a childhood memory, insisting that the bells must never be rung after nightfall.

### 03 | The Backward Tracks
Fresh bootprints cross a muddy path, heel-first, as though someone walked backward for miles. At the final print sits a sealed lunch basket, still warm. A scrap of paper inside says, in cramped handwriting, 'Please do not follow me until tomorrow.'

### 04 | The Lost Surveyor
A surveyor is setting out red flags through an empty field. Her map shows a river where the field is now. She asks the party to help locate a bridge that vanished last month, promising payment from a town nobody nearby has heard of.

### 05 | A Door in the Hill
A narrow red door has been painted onto a chalk hillside. It has a working brass handle, cold enough to frost the fingers. From the other side comes the patient sound of a person sweeping a floor, but the hillside is solid for many yards.

### 06 | The Fox Procession
A line of foxes emerges from a pinewood carrying twigs in their mouths. Each drops its twig at the feet of the tallest traveler. The final fox wears a collar engraved with the party's destination, spelled correctly except for one letter.

### 07 | The Unfinished Bridge
A new wooden bridge spans a dry ravine, but its final plank has never been nailed down. Three builders sit nearby with untouched lunches. Every time one attempts to finish the bridge, a distant voice announces that the work has already been completed.

### 08 | A Map for Sale
A barefoot child offers an exquisitely detailed map of the next day's journey, including weather and campfire smoke. The child will accept only an unusual button as payment. One corner of the map has been folded over and sealed with wax.

### 09 | The Glass Pond
A pond lies perfectly still despite a hard wind. Beneath its surface, the party can see a star-filled night sky and a small boat passing slowly overhead. A nearby fisherman has not caught a fish, but his bucket contains several wet feathers.

### 10 | The Listening Stones
At a crossroads, a pile of boundary stones whispers when approached. The whispering ceases whenever anyone speaks aloud. A local shepherd swears the stones were carried here one by one by travelers who later returned to hear what they had left behind.

### 11 | Three Empty Saddles
Three saddled horses wander out of the trees, calm and well fed. Their saddlebags contain travel permits bearing the party's names, dated next week. The horses obey ordinary commands, but every animal refuses to cross a particular patch of yellow grass.

### 12 | The Funeral Without a Body
A small procession advances along the road with an empty bier and a single black candle. The mourners say they are burying the name of a mountain, not a person. One asks the travelers to remember the name, then insists no one say it aloud.

### 13 | The Collector of Rain
A woman in a patched coat hangs delicate bottles from tree branches. Each contains rain collected on a different date. She offers one bottle freely, but warns that uncorking it will briefly summon the weather from the day written on its label.

### 14 | The Mill in the Mist
The sound of a watermill comes from a valley with no stream. In the mist, its wheel turns steadily and flour leaks from an upstairs window. A miller asks whether the party has seen his daughter, who went to fetch water during last winter's thaw.

### 15 | The Road's New Name
Fresh wooden markers at the edge of the trail display a new name for the road. Every traveler who reads it aloud pronounces a different name, and all insist theirs is written plainly. A merchant offers an alarming sum for an untouched marker.

### 16 | A Camp of Statues
A dozen stone figures sit around the ashes of a campfire, posed in mid-conversation. Their plates are full of fresh supper. A kettle still bubbles over warm coals, and one stone figure holds a carving knife with an ordinary wooden handle.

### 17 | The Singing Burrow
Soft singing rises from a rabbit burrow beneath a hawthorn. The melody follows the party's footsteps exactly, accelerating when they hurry. A hunter claims the song can lead lost children home, provided nobody tries to identify the singer.

### 18 | The Disputed Spring
Two villages' water carriers argue beside a clear spring. Each claims the spring belongs upstream of the other's boundary, although its water runs in neither direction. Their argument has lasted since dawn, and every filled bucket immediately becomes heavier than expected.

### 19 | The Giant's Needle
A sewing needle as tall as a person stands upright in an abandoned meadow. A thread of pale green silk vanishes into the ground. Someone has attached a note apologizing for the inconvenience and requesting that the needle not be moved before sunset.

### 20 | The Patient Wolf
A lean gray wolf sits beside the road with one forepaw raised. It does not growl or flee. Around its neck hangs a small leather pouch containing a clean bandage, a silver coin, and a note written in a child's carefully practiced hand.

### 21 | The False Sunrise
Before dawn, a brilliant sunrise appears between two distant hills, then goes dark after only a minute. Birds briefly begin their morning calls and fall silent again. An astronomer in the party's path is frantically packing instruments, apparently pleased rather than frightened.

### 22 | The Salt Merchant
A cart laden with salt blocks has overturned in the mud. The driver refuses help until everyone promises not to taste the cargo. One block bears a trapped leaf, green and unwithered. Another contains a small shadow that moves independently of the light.

### 23 | A Very Small Toll
A narrow bridge is guarded by an old woman demanding one word from each traveler as payment. She writes the words in a ledger and never asks for money. A nearby sign lists words already taken, including several that the party may need later.

### 24 | The Absent Village
The party reaches a village shown on every map, but only its chimneys remain. Smoke curls from each, though no houses stand beneath them. Freshly swept doorsteps mark the old street. Somewhere behind a missing wall, a baby begins to laugh.

### 25 | The Sparrow Messenger
A sparrow lands on a traveler's pack and repeatedly taps a single buckle. Inside its beak is a tiny rolled parchment bearing a local innkeeper's seal. The note asks the recipient to stop carrying an object the party does not remember acquiring.

### 26 | The Bright Cairn
One stone in a roadside cairn is polished to a mirror sheen. Its reflection shows the landscape as it was decades ago, with a tower standing nearby. The other stones are ordinary, except for a fresh one marked with today's date.

### 27 | The Walking Garden
A carefully tended vegetable garden is moving slowly across a meadow on hundreds of pale roots. The scarecrow turns to watch the travelers pass. At the rear, a furious gardener pursues it with a watering can and a wheelbarrow full of soil.

### 28 | Snow in the Hollow
A small hollow is full of new snow in midsummer. Bare footprints lead into it and vanish. At its center sits a basket of warm bread, dusted with white powder that proves to be flour rather than snow when touched.

### 29 | The Horse That Knows the Way
A riderless mare waits at a fork, already bridled. She accepts food but pulls away from every attempt to lead her west. The saddle bears a recently carved map of a shortcut, with a small cross scratched beside an unnamed pond.

### 30 | The River's Apology
A river has changed course overnight, abandoning a busy ford. On the damp riverbed lies a line of smooth stones spelling out an apology in the local language. Fishermen disagree about whether the message is a miracle or a remarkably elaborate prank.

### 31 | The Windless Flags
Colorful pennants hang from poles around an empty tournament ground. None move, though the grass lies flat in a strong wind. At the center is a prize cup engraved with tomorrow's date and the name of a contestant nobody can locate.

### 32 | The Houseboat on Dry Land
An elegant houseboat sits stranded on a grassy ridge, ropes neatly secured to fence posts. Its owner serves tea on deck and insists the river will return shortly. The last flood in this country took place a century ago.

### 33 | The Beekeeper's Warning
A beekeeper carries an empty hive wrapped in black cloth. She warns travelers not to follow any lone bee traveling against the wind. Before she can explain, one solitary golden bee begins circling the smallest belonging in the party's luggage.

### 34 | The Sleeping Tollkeeper
An armored tollkeeper sleeps upright in a booth beside a ruined road. Whenever anyone approaches, a bell rings underground. He wakes only long enough to ask the party to deliver a letter addressed to the person who will eventually replace him.

### 35 | The Invisible Flock
Hundreds of tiny bells and hoofbeats move across an empty hillside. Grass bends under unseen bodies. A shepherd carries an ordinary crook and chats amiably with the air. He offers fresh cheese, but requests the travelers keep their dogs close.

### 36 | A Question from the Well
An old stone well whispers each traveler's name as they approach. Its next question is different for everyone, and nobody else can hear it. The bucket at the bottom is full of dry leaves despite standing far below the waterline.

### 37 | The Mountain Postbox
A red iron postbox stands alone on a high pass. It contains letters addressed to people who died long ago and one to someone presently traveling with the party. The latest collection stamp is dated seven minutes into the future.

### 38 | The Candle Tree
A dead oak bears dozens of beeswax candles in place of leaves. A small flame appears on one branch whenever someone tells a lie nearby. A wandering monk has slept beneath it for nights and cannot understand why his candle never goes out.

### 39 | The Friendly Ambush
Armed figures spring from roadside bushes and demand the party remain still. Before anyone can react, the bandits hurriedly lower their weapons. They explain that they are rehearsing an ambush for a wedding performance and ask for a professional critique.

### 40 | The Lost Weathercock
A copper weathercock shaped like a dragon has fallen beside the path. Its arrow turns steadily to point at the closest lie being told nearby. A priest arrives breathless, insisting that the device belongs on a tower visible only from the other side of the valley.

### 41 | The Silver Reeds
A bed of silver reeds grows beside a black pond. When touched, each reed produces the voice of a different absent person. One voice offers clear directions to the nearest town; another urgently asks whether the listener is standing in water.

### 42 | The Cart of Empty Frames
A peddler pushes a cart full of empty picture frames. He offers to hang one around any landscape the party wishes to remember. Framed scenery remains brightly colored while everything outside fades for a moment into exhausted gray.

### 43 | The Stranger's Campfire
A small campfire burns in the rain beside neatly stacked firewood. There is no traveler in sight. A pot of stew simmers, and two bowls have been set out for guests. In the mud, all footprints lead away from the fire.

### 44 | A Crown of Moss
A deer with a crown of green moss stands quietly in the road. It carries a brass key tangled in its antlers. A hunter kneels when he sees it and begs the party not to mention the deer to anyone in the nearest village.

### 45 | The Borrowed Road
A newly paved road curves through the forest, smooth as a city street. It ends abruptly in a clearing where a bewildered work crew is searching for its stolen tools. Their survey says the new road should be several miles to the north.

### 46 | The Book of Mushrooms
A mushroom collector reads from a small damp book whose pages are blank to everyone else. She identifies each fungus by a name associated with a secret. She offers one medicinal mushroom in exchange for a promise never to reveal where it grew.

### 47 | The White Kite
A white kite circles high above the trail despite the lack of wind. Its string descends into an old grave, vanishing through the earth. A nearby child has been trying to retrieve it and says someone below keeps gently tugging back.

### 48 | The Millstone Race
A pair of enormous millstones roll side by side down a distant lane. Villagers cheer at a roadside fence, placing bets on which will reach the bridge first. The stones move without anyone pushing, and neither leaves a mark on the road.

### 49 | The Merchant of Echoes
A voice from a ravine offers to sell echoes from distant places. For a copper coin tossed down, it returns the sound of ocean surf, market bells, or children's laughter. The seller refuses to show themselves, claiming they have sold their own footsteps.

### 50 | A Stranger's Harvest
A field of ripe wheat has grown overnight in the middle of an old battlefield. The heads bow toward the nearest living person. A farmer is already harvesting it, though he insists he neither planted the grain nor intends to eat it.

### 51 | The Blackwater Ferry
A ferryman waits beside a river too shallow for a boat. He offers passage anyway, but will take passengers only one at a time. His craft casts a shadow much larger than the vessel, and the opposite bank looks different through its window.

### 52 | The Glassmaker's Trail
Along the trail, ordinary stones have melted into glossy blue glass without signs of fire. The marks stretch for miles. At the newest patch, a glassmaker kneels to examine a perfectly preserved pawprint inside the molten surface.

### 53 | The Brass Insects
Tiny clockwork beetles emerge from a fallen log and begin arranging pebbles into patterns. Each pattern resembles a map of the nearby woods, but includes a building not present in the landscape. Their ticking stops when the party approaches too closely.

### 54 | The Missing Noon
The shadow of a standing stone leaps suddenly from morning to afternoon without the sun moving. A caravan refuses to travel past it. Their guide insists that anyone walking through the stone's shadow will forget what happened during the intervening hour.

### 55 | The Hunter's Debt
A hunter offers to trade a beautifully carved bow for a loaf of ordinary bread. He claims it has never missed, which is why he has become afraid to use it. His companion quietly asks the party not to accept anything bearing the hunter's initials.

### 56 | The Stolen Bridge Toll
An elderly bridgekeeper demands payment for a bridge that has been washed away. He can produce an official receipt and a compelling account of the tolls his predecessors collected. Behind him, stones are beginning to rise slowly from the riverbed.

### 57 | The Bluefire Camp
A recently abandoned campsite contains a cooking fire burning blue and giving no heat. Its flames make nearby writing legible even on blank paper. Pinned to a tree is a message reading, 'Please keep it burning until we come back.'

### 58 | A Traveler with Two Names
A friendly traveler introduces himself, then politely corrects himself using a completely different name. His documents agree with neither introduction. He seems genuinely puzzled by the discrepancy and asks the party to choose which name he should use.

### 59 | The Timekeeper's Nest
A tree is hung with broken pocket watches, some still ticking. Inside a magpie's nest lies an ornate hourglass with no sand. A repairer below the tree offers a reward for one particular watch that has not yet been stolen.

### 60 | The Marsh Lanterns
A row of lanterns shines from a marsh at dusk, marking what appears to be a safe path. At the first lantern rests a pair of dry boots and a walking stick. Muddy footprints travel away from the light in every direction.

### 61 | The Umbrella Orchard
A sudden shower breaks over the road, although the sky remains clear. At a roadside stall, a merchant rents umbrellas made of enormous waxed leaves. She insists each umbrella is numbered and must be returned before the rain reaches its owner's house.

### 62 | The Borrowed Riverstone
A traveler is searching desperately for a smooth gray stone lost from his pocket. He says the stone is responsible for keeping the nearby stream in its bed. The stream is already climbing the bank in narrow fingers of water.

### 63 | The Giant's Footprint
A single enormous footprint appears in damp earth beside the road. It contains an ordinary campsite, neatly arranged and recently used. The campers claim they woke to find the footprint around them and have been afraid to cross its edge.

### 64 | A Feathered Funeral
Blackbirds gather around a dead tree in absolute silence. Beneath it lies a small handcart covered with feathers. A traveling undertaker says the cart belongs to a bird that once carried messages for a king, though he cannot explain why it needed wheels.

### 65 | The Invisible Market
Coins clink and voices haggle beneath a grove of empty trees. Goods appear briefly when their prices are spoken aloud. A merchant whispers that the market trades in opportunities, and warns that bargaining without naming your offer counts as acceptance.

### 66 | The Last Green Leaf
Every tree in the valley has turned gold except for one bright green leaf on a bare branch. A small crowd gathers beneath it, taking turns holding a ladder. Nobody can agree whether they are waiting for it to fall or protecting it from falling.

### 67 | The Rope Across the Sky
A thick ship's rope stretches between two distant hilltops, suspended high above the road. It hums in the wind. A sailor beneath it is trying to find the ship it came from and offers a reward for news of a vessel with no sea.

### 68 | The Antlered Messenger
A tall figure wearing a stag's skull waits at the trail's edge holding a sealed letter. The figure refuses to speak but points insistently toward the horizon. The letter is addressed to the next person who makes a decision for the party.

### 69 | The Lost Shepherd's Star
A shepherd shows the party a small, cold star trapped in a glass jar. It flickers whenever the northern horizon darkens. He says he found it after last night's meteor shower and fears that something large may come looking for it.

### 70 | The Orchard's Guest
Beneath a pear tree, a finely dressed guest has set a table for six, complete with wine and polished silverware. She welcomes the travelers to her annual reunion. All the other place cards bear names scratched out so heavily they cannot be read.

### 71 | The Path of Shells
Fresh seashells line a mountain path, becoming more numerous as the travelers climb. A stranded crab sits in a puddle at the summit. From beyond the next ridge comes the faint, unmistakable sound of waves breaking against a shore.

### 72 | The Traveling Hearth
A squat iron stove walks slowly down the road on four clawed legs. Its chimney smokes pleasantly, and warm bread can be smelled through the door. Every time someone looks inside, a different landscape appears beyond the embers.

### 73 | The Silent Contest
Two archers stand opposite one another in a clearing, bows raised but arrows still nocked. Between them lies a single red ribbon. Neither will shoot until a neutral observer declares the wind favorable, and the wind has been perfectly still since yesterday.

### 74 | The Sunken Library
A flooded stone cellar opens beside the path. Beneath clear water, books rest untouched on shelves. One volume lies open and its pages turn whenever a traveler steps closer. The title is familiar, though nobody recalls ever seeing a copy.

### 75 | The Talking Milestone
A roadside milestone recites the distance to nearby settlements whenever touched. The distances are accurate, except that one destination is reported as getting nearer with every passing minute, even when the travelers stand motionless. Faint footsteps can be heard under the stone.

### 76 | The Salt-White Deer
A white deer drinks from a pool surrounded by dead grass. Instead of fleeing, it turns and watches the party with unnerving calm. Wherever its hooves press the mud, tiny white flowers immediately grow, then fold shut before anyone can pick them.

### 77 | The Orchard Keeper's Clock
An orchard keeper asks the travelers to hold a pocket watch while he climbs a ladder. Whenever it is opened, an apple falls somewhere nearby. He claims the watch belonged to his grandmother, who could always tell when a harvest was ready.

### 78 | The Mountain's Breath
At regular intervals, a soft warm wind exhales from a crack in the mountainside. Between breaths, every nearby sound seems louder than it should. A miner listening at the fissure says the mountain is trying to pronounce something but cannot manage the final consonant.

### 79 | The Threadbare Banner
A ragged banner hangs from a lone lance thrust into a hill. Its emblem changes slightly whenever the travelers look away. A soldier in mismatched armor arrives and asks whether they have seen the rest of his regiment, which set out to find this flag a week ago.

### 80 | The Last Footbridge
The party reaches a narrow footbridge whose planks are painted with tiny constellations. A carpenter sits nearby replacing a single board. She says the bridge grows shorter each night and asks the travelers to cross now, before it forgets the other bank.
