# Creatures Behaving Strangely

## Production brief
Each numbered entry is an independent, system-neutral encounter. Numbers and headings are production metadata, never spoken. A listener seeks into the encounter portion, ignores the fragment underway, and uses the next encounter heard from its beginning. No electronic cues are used. Do not prescribe combat, rewards, or an explanation. Suggested production: Brother Rubicon, one quiet introductory observation, then “I am Brother Rubicon. The die's cast.” with a 0.3-second beat on either side of that complete sentence. No fixed-duration claim until the narration is rendered and measured. The ending is not an encounter.

**Episode identity:** Animals whose conduct presents a choice, a question, or a strange obligation.
**Opening observation:** Animals are said to know things before we do. This does not mean they intend to tell us.
**Closing coda (not an encounter):** The animals have gone quiet. I do not believe that means they have finished.

## Encounter manuscript (80 independent results)

### 01 | The Wolf's Appointment
A wolf waits outside a village gate every evening at the same hour. It brings a different household object each time. Tonight it carries a baby's shoe, carefully cleaned.

### 02 | The Deer Who Counts
A deer stands beside a narrow bridge and stamps its hoof once for every traveler crossing. If someone turns back, it starts counting again. A local shepherd says the deer has never reached seven.

### 03 | The Courteous Crows
Six crows are placing bright pebbles in a circle around a sleeping dog. They step aside whenever anyone approaches, then resume their work. The dog appears entirely unconcerned.

### 04 | The Fox's Errand
A fox carrying a sealed letter follows the road but stops at every fork, waiting for someone to point. The seal bears a familiar crest, although the letter is addressed only to 'Whoever knows the way.'

### 05 | The Horses at Council
Five riderless horses stand in a ring, occasionally stamping as though taking turns speaking. One wears a magistrate's ceremonial chain. When approached, the horses separate into two opposing groups.

### 06 | The Patient Bear
A bear sits beneath a pear tree, holding a fallen fruit in both paws without eating it. Every few minutes it places the pear on a stone and looks toward the road. A small empty basket hangs from a branch.

### 07 | The Owl in Daylight
An owl has taken up residence on a market sign and refuses to sleep. It watches only people carrying mirrors. A shopkeeper claims the owl arrived the morning every mirror in town fogged over.

### 08 | The Fish on the Hill
A silver fish lies alive in a puddle at the summit of a dry hill. Each morning the puddle is farther uphill. Someone has marked its progress with small flags.

### 09 | The Ants' Procession
A procession of ants carries a tiny crown across a kitchen floor. Other ants follow with a folded scrap of black cloth. The householder asks the travelers not to interfere with the funeral.

### 10 | The Stag's Reflection
A stag drinks from a stream, but its reflection remains standing after the animal walks away. The reflected stag looks upstream, where a child is building a dam from stones.

### 11 | The Geese on Watch
A flock of geese forms a perfect line across a lane. They allow people through one at a time, but turn back anyone carrying an unopened parcel. The nearby postmaster is furious.

### 12 | The Cat's Witness
A black cat attends every hearing in a village court. It sits beside the accused until testimony ends, then moves to the judge. Today it has refused to enter the room.

### 13 | The Snail's Trail
A snail crosses a wall leaving a shining trail that forms legible words. The message is a list of ingredients for a meal nobody recognizes. The final ingredient is a date.

### 14 | The Moth Collector
Hundreds of moths gather on a ruined door at night, arranging themselves into the outline of a key. By morning the keyhole is blocked with fresh wax.

### 15 | The Dog Who Returns
A stray dog repeatedly brings the same stick to a traveler, no matter how far it is thrown. On the fourth return the stick is carved with a name that was not there before.

### 16 | The Sheep's Circle
A flock of sheep grazes in a perfect ring around a patch of untouched grass. The shepherd has tried moving them, but they reform the circle wherever they go. At its center is a warm stone.

### 17 | The Heron's Bargain
A heron stands beside a fisherman and drops a coin into his empty bucket after every missed catch. The fisherman insists he has never seen the bird before. The coins bear portraits of different rulers.

### 18 | The Bees' Choice
A swarm of bees settles gently on one traveler's pack, ignoring flowers and food. They form a narrow arrow pointing to a nearby cottage. Its windows have been boarded from inside.

### 19 | The Tortoise's Race
A tortoise walks steadily down a deserted road with a red ribbon tied around its shell. Behind it, a series of finish lines has been painted on the stones. A breathless runner asks how far ahead it is.

### 20 | The Ravens' Rehearsal
Two ravens take turns imitating the same human voice, correcting each other after every attempt. They have mastered the words 'Not that door.' Neither seems willing to say which door they mean.

### 21 | The Hedgehog's Collection
A hedgehog arranges discarded thimbles in a hollow log, each packed with moss. One is made of gold and bears a royal seal. When a traveler approaches, the animal covers that thimble first.

### 22 | The Bees at the Well
Bees enter an abandoned well without carrying pollen. They emerge with tiny droplets of clear water and deposit them on a carved stone. The water is slowly forming letters.

### 23 | The Otter's Toll
An otter waits at a river crossing with smooth stones laid on the bank. It pushes one into the water for each traveler crossing. The last stone is set apart and painted blue.

### 24 | The Mice in Uniform
A dozen mice gather beneath a kitchen table, each wearing a colored thread. They take turns climbing a spoon propped against a cup. The household cat sits nearby, wearing a matching ribbon.

### 25 | The Horse That Refuses Home
A farmer's horse stands at an open stable door, refusing to enter. It readily pulls a cart or grazes, but rears whenever the farmer brings out a particular bridle. The bridle is new.

### 26 | The Frog Choir
Frogs sing in flawless harmony from a roadside ditch. They fall silent when anyone looks directly at them. One voice continues a moment longer, though the only frog visible there is made of stone.

### 27 | The Mole's Message
A mole appears beside a newly dug grave and pushes up a silver button. It returns with a second, then a third. Each button matches a coat belonging to someone still alive.

### 28 | The Lizard at the Sundial
A green lizard lies in a sundial's shadow, moving around its base as the shadow moves. When clouds cover the sun, it chooses a position anyway. The clockkeeper says it has never been wrong.

### 29 | The Rabbit's Audience
A rabbit sits on a stump before a row of empty stools. It thumps its foot three times, waits, and thumps again. From somewhere in the grass comes a single answering knock.

### 30 | The Silent Rooster
The village rooster has stopped crowing but still climbs the fence at dawn. Each morning it leaves an object on the top rail: a feather, a nail, a dry rose. Today it has brought a key.

### 31 | The Spider's Alphabet
A spider has woven a web across an abandoned shop's doorway. Its strands form letters in a language the shopkeeper recognizes from childhood. Each morning another word appears, and one old word is missing.

### 32 | The Salamander's Fire
A salamander sleeps in the cold ashes of a hearth. When the cook lights a fire elsewhere, it wakes and walks toward the flames. On the wall, tiny soot marks show how far it has traveled.

### 33 | The Goat's Burden
A goat leads three others through a market, each carrying an empty saddlebag. Shopkeepers stop work to offer scraps of parchment. The lead goat accepts some and solemnly rejects the rest.

### 34 | The Raven's Substitute
A raven sits in a judge's window every morning, listening to petitioners. Since the judge fell ill, it has begun dropping small stones into two bowls. The clerk is recording the totals.

### 35 | The Carp of the Orchard
In a flooded orchard, a large carp swims between apple trees. It surfaces beneath one tree every afternoon and waits while fruit falls into the water. Today it has surfaced beneath the only tree without apples.

### 36 | The Donkey's Compass
A donkey hauling an empty cart stops at crossroads and turns toward the least-used path. The driver lets it choose once. Hours later they arrive at the driver's childhood house, which burned down long ago.

### 37 | The Swallows' Repairs
Swallows repair a collapsed bridge with mud, straw, and pieces of blue glass. Their nests are elsewhere. An engineer laughs at the tiny work until she notices which stones the birds refuse to touch.

### 38 | The Crab in the Courtyard
A crab crosses a dry courtyard every morning carrying seaweed. It deposits the weed beneath the same locked door and returns toward the coast. No one admits to having brought it inland.

### 39 | The Viper's Watch
A viper lies coiled around a bronze ring half-buried by the roadside. It permits travelers to pass. Every time thunder sounds, it raises its head toward a distant tower.

### 40 | The Three Antlers
Three deer stand in a clearing, each with a single antler. They approach one another and lower their heads as though attempting to fit together. In the grass lies a fourth antler, newly polished.

### 41 | The Dog That Knows the Song
A shepherd's dog refuses ordinary commands but comes immediately when anyone whistles a particular lullaby. The shepherd has never heard the tune. The dog wears a collar embroidered with a date in the future.

### 42 | The Sparrow's Ledger
A sparrow flies between two merchants, dropping a seed into a jar whenever one speaks. By midday the jar is full. Neither merchant knows who placed it there, but both have stopped exaggerating.

### 43 | The Eel in the Fountain
An eel lives in a public fountain and coils around the spout whenever a particular bell rings. The water stops until the bell falls silent. The keeper insists no pipe is large enough for the eel.

### 44 | The Sheep Who Stays Behind
Each evening one sheep remains in the field, facing an old milestone after the flock has gone. The shepherd counts the animals: always correct, until the sheep by the stone rejoins them.

### 45 | The Butterfly's Mirror
A white butterfly lands on a polished shield, then returns to an overgrown gate. When someone turns the shield to follow it, the reflection shows a gate standing open, though the real gate remains shut.

### 46 | The Peacock's Warning
A peacock displays its tail whenever someone mentions the mayor's name. It closes the feathers at every other name, even when offered food. The mayor's daughter has arrived to ask whether anyone has seen the bird.

### 47 | The Snakeskin Trader
A grass snake leaves its shed skin on a doorstep every seventh day. The skins grow longer, but the snake remains small. An apothecary offers a fortune for the next one and refuses to say why.

### 48 | The Hound's Empty Leash
A hound crosses a bridge each afternoon dragging an empty leash. At the far end it sits and waits for a signal nobody else hears. The tollkeeper claims a second set of footsteps always accompanies it.

### 49 | The Woodpecker's Choice
A woodpecker taps seven trees in succession but never the eighth. Its tapping draws carpenters who recognize the rhythm as the hammering heard from a cellar sealed for decades.

### 50 | The Seal on the Riverbank
A young seal lies beside a freshwater river far from the sea. A neat stack of stones stands beside it, wet with saltwater. It nudges a new stone toward anyone who offers to help it home.

### 51 | The Magpie's Apology
A magpie steals a ribbon from a child's hair, then returns it tied around a small bone. Over the next week, stolen objects reappear on the same doorstep. Each has been carefully repaired.

### 52 | The Vole's Tunnel
A vole digs a tunnel in a perfect circle, ending where it began. It repeats the circle every dawn, each time slightly larger. At the center stands a flower that blooms only after sunset.

### 53 | The Bats in the Belfry
At sunset the bats leave the belfry one at a time and circle the village before returning. A watchman counts eleven out and ten back. This evening one remains outside, waiting beneath a lantern.

### 54 | The Stork's Parcel
A stork has built its nest from folded letters rather than twigs. Most are unopened and addressed to distant towns. One envelope has been placed carefully in the street, addressed to a traveler passing through.

### 55 | The Wolf Who Brings Wood
Each winter morning a wolf leaves a branch at an isolated cottage. The widow inside burns it in her stove. Yesterday the wolf brought a branch still bearing green leaves, though the forest has been frozen for months.

### 56 | The Patient Dragonfly
A dragonfly hovers above a narrow stream, holding its position against the wind. It turns whenever someone says 'tomorrow.' A child is trying to teach it other words, but it has learned only one.

### 57 | The Goose's Missing Egg
A goose has laid a circle of ordinary eggs around a smooth stone. It hisses when anyone approaches the stone but permits its eggs to be collected. The stone is warmer than the eggs.

### 58 | The Ferret's Map
A ferret carries fragments of torn paper into a barn. They are pieces of a map, arranged beneath a broken ladder. Each fragment comes from a different owner's pocket.

### 59 | The Toad at the Gate
A fat toad sits on the threshold of an abandoned garden. Anyone who steps around it hears a faint cough from behind the locked gate. The toad moves aside only for someone carrying a flower.

### 60 | The Heron's Second Shadow
A heron stands motionless in shallow water. Its shadow flies overhead while the bird remains on the bank. Fishermen say the shadow returns each evening carrying something that falls soundlessly into the reeds.

### 61 | The Cat Who Leaves Breadcrumbs
A cat patrols the village at night, dropping breadcrumbs from a pouch around its neck. The trail ends at a painted-over cellar door. By morning the crumbs are gone, but the pouch is full again.

### 62 | The Crickets' Rest
Every cricket in a meadow falls silent at precisely midnight for one full minute. A farmer tried shouting during the silence. Last night something beneath the soil whispered his name.

### 63 | The Mare at the Mirror
A white mare stands before a mirror in a ruined stable, occasionally stamping at her reflection. In the mirror she wears a saddle and a rider. The rider is holding out an empty hand.

### 64 | The Kingfisher's Gift
A kingfisher drops a bright blue feather at a fisherman's feet after each storm. The fisherman has collected twelve. Laid in a row, the feathers point toward different bends in the river.

### 65 | The Snail on the Bell
A snail climbs the town bell and settles beneath the clapper. The bell rings but the snail never falls. Since the snail arrived, the sexton says the bell strikes an extra time at noon.

### 66 | The Beetles' Burial
Dark beetles carry a sliver of polished glass toward a mound of earth. Each time they reach the top, it rolls back down. A village child insists they are trying to bury a reflection.

### 67 | The Fox at the Window
A fox appears at a cottage window each evening carrying an unfamiliar object. The occupant names it aloud and the fox departs. Tonight it brings a portrait of someone nobody in the cottage recognizes.

### 68 | The Bees in the Clock
A clockmaker finds bees living inside a grandfather clock. They make no honey but have packed the pendulum case with wax seals. The clock keeps time only when the front door is open.

### 69 | The Lamb's Escort
A black lamb follows a funeral procession without joining the grazing flock nearby. It stops at each crossroads until someone speaks the deceased's name. At the cemetery gate, it turns back toward town.

### 70 | The Badger's Roadwork
A badger digs shallow trenches across the road at even intervals. A surveyor notices they match distances on an old map. The final trench lies directly under the surveyor's own camp.

### 71 | The Swan's Request
A swan pulls strips of red cloth from the reeds and arranges them along the lakeshore. Every strip belongs to a missing banner. When the wind changes, the swan rearranges them.

### 72 | The Moth at the Window
A single moth visits an inn's upper window and beats its wings against the glass exactly three times. The innkeeper has begun leaving the window open. The moth still knocks before entering.

### 73 | The Tortoise and the Milestone
An old tortoise sleeps with its shell pressed against a milestone. Travelers notice the mile number changes while it sleeps. When the animal wakes, the distance returns to normal.

### 74 | The Crow's Bargain
A crow places a nut at the feet of anyone leaving the village. If taken, the bird follows for exactly one mile. At the boundary it retrieves the nut, whether or not the traveler still has it.

### 75 | The Sheepdog's Other Flock
A sheepdog herds fallen leaves into a circle, then lies beside them until dawn. The shepherd cannot persuade it to stop. One morning the circle contains a single white woolen thread.

### 76 | The Trout's Petition
A trout leaps from the stream to strike a low branch, knocking loose a leaf each time. The leaves drift toward a mill whose wheel has been still for years. The miller has begun collecting them.

### 77 | The Owl's Small Library
An owl brings scraps of printed paper to its nest and tucks them beneath the twigs. The scraps are pages from books missing from a nearby school. Every scrap contains the same word.

### 78 | The Raven's Appointment
A raven waits each day atop an empty gallows and gives one call at noon. When a carpenter begins dismantling the gallows, the raven moves to the bell tower and calls twice.

### 79 | The Fireflies' Boundary
Fireflies illuminate a precise rectangle above a fallow field, leaving its interior completely dark. A farmer says a house once stood there. The insects change the outline whenever someone steps inside.

### 80 | The Beaver's Bridge
A beaver constructs a narrow wooden bridge over a stream where nobody needs to cross. It rejects fallen branches and selects only pieces marked with human handwriting. The bridge ends at a locked garden gate.
