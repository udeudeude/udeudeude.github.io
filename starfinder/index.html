Art belongs to the layout

The toolkit includes copy-paste patterns for full-width scene art, transparent character cutouts, half-page and full-page plates, inset illustrations, edge art, maps, and chapter-opening images.

Current examples use Paizo-hosted Starfinder 2e iconic art where the applicable fan/community-use terms permit it.

How to use it

  1. Download the .md file.
  2. Open a blank Homebrewery brew.
  3. Delete the starter material and paste the entire toolkit source.
  4. Copy the components you need into your own brew and replace demo text/art.

Do not use “New from File.” Plain copy-paste has been the reliable path in testing.

Why it is a toolkit, not just a skin

The recurring visual forms correspond to recurring Starfinder authoring problems. A component earns its place only if it makes information easier to write, find, compare, or run at the table.

That rule is the main lesson from the successful Tales from the Loop toolkit and the abandoned Old Gods of Appalachia attempt.
