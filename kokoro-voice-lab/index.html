04 Presets & export

Your two named “Saved” presets are permanently included in this app and backed up as JSON on GitHub, so clearing browser data won't erase them. Additional “Save preset locally” storage is specific to this browser and device.

The WAV includes the current effects. Audio stays in your browser; only your settings are saved locally when you ask. On iPhone, use the Share menu to save the WAV to Files. To keep this app handy, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

05 What am I hearing?

Pitch

How quickly a voice's source vibrates. Changes its musical height.

Formants

Frequency areas emphasized by a vocal tract. They distinguish vowels and shape perceived voice size.

Tempo

How fast the words pass. It can change without forcing the pitch upward or downward.

EQ

Broadly boosts or cuts existing frequencies. It doesn't actively move the voice's original vowel resonances.

Under the hood: The voice-shaping preview uses an independent time-stretch/repitch stage and a cepstral spectral-envelope shifter. It is experimental; big changes can sound synthetic or smeared, especially on fricatives. For final episode masters we can reproduce your chosen settings with higher-quality studio processing.
