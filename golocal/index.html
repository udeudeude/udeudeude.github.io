Asheville GoLocal Card + Google Maps

An experiment in making the Go Local directory easier to use alongside an everyday map.

What it does

The original Mac-assisted collection built a Google Maps Saved list named Go Local Card, marking participating businesses with a 💳 icon. The collection located 413 physical, mappable places from the Go Local directory at the time. Once that list is in a Google account, it also appears in Google Maps on an iPhone signed into the same account.

Privacy first: This site's creator is not sharing a list from a personal Google account. Google says a shared Google Maps list can display the creator's Google Account name and profile photo. A shareable list link is therefore deliberately not published here yet. This site cannot add places to your Google account automatically.

For someone receiving the shared list

  1. Open the shared Go Local Card Google Maps link on your phone.
  2. Sign into Google Maps with the account you normally use.
  3. Tap Save list (or Follow, depending on your version of Maps).
  4. Open the Google Maps app and go to You / Saved to find the list among the lists you have saved.
  5. If its markers are hidden, use the list's three-dot menu and choose Show on your map.

Google documents this feature in its official instructions for lists on iPhone and iPad.

If you already imported the list on a Mac

  1. On the Mac, open Google Maps in the account where you ran the importer and find your Go Local Card Saved list.
  2. Open Google Maps on your phone with that same Google account.
  3. Go to You / Saved, find Go Local Card, and ensure Show on your map is enabled.

This is a synced Saved list, not a separate app and not a Google My Maps overlay.

A public list without exposing a personal account

Google states that shared lists show the Google Account's name and profile picture. A separate custom name for public Maps contributions is not a reliable privacy safeguard for list sharing. Don't publish the existing private list's share link, even temporarily.

  1. Create a dedicated Google account for this independent project, without your personal name, photo, or other personal details in its public-facing profile.
  2. Recreate the Go Local Card Saved list within that account from the project data. Google Maps doesn't provide a simple bulk transfer between accounts, so verify which places and notes can be migrated before proceeding.
  3. Check the dedicated account's public profile and open a test share link from an unrelated, signed-out browser or second account. Review the list name, owner name/photo, place notes, and any collaborators.
  4. Only then publish the public project's view-only share URL here as Save Go Local list in Google Maps. Do not enable collaborative editing for strangers.

Useful official references: how your name and photo appear on Google Maps and how to save and share lists on iPhone.

Until a dedicated public account and verified list are available, this page provides instructions, not a direct installation button.

About the data

The project originally harvested 413 mappable listings; this is a snapshot, not a continuously maintained official business directory. Locations, card participation, and offers may have changed since collection.

Independent experiment. Not an official Go Local Asheville or Google project. For current participants, use the official Go Local directory.
